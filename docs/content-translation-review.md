# Content translation review

The lesson resources use Czech as their source of truth. English and German are generated once at build time and bundled as static dictionaries; no translation request is made by the browser.

These terms were flagged for a human language review because their best wording depends on the intended therapeutic vocabulary. They are translated in the first pass so no lesson falls back to Czech.

| Czech source term | English first pass | German first pass | Why review it | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| vnitřní kompas | inner compass | innerer Kompass | The product name **Vnitřní kompas** remains unchanged, while generic references may be descriptive. | Keep the brand spelling; decide whether generic uses should also stay branded. |
| zachytit / zachycený signál | notice / noticed signal | wahrnehmen / wahrgenommenes Signal | “Capture” is literal but can sound technical in wellbeing copy. | Prefer “notice” / “wahrnehmen” when referring to a bodily or emotional cue. |
| převzaté pravidlo | inherited rule | übernommene Regel | “Inherited” can suggest family history; the source also includes learned social rules. | Review whether “learned rule” / “übernommene Regel” best matches Kristian’s intent. |
| potřeba se ozývá | a need is making itself known | ein Bedürfnis macht sich bemerkbar | The Czech is intentionally embodied and idiomatic. | Keep the natural idiom rather than a clinical equivalent. |
| odchýlení / automat | drift / automatic pattern | Abweichung / Automatismus | These words describe a recurring self-observation pattern, not a diagnosis. | Confirm preferred terminology across the final editorial glossary. |
| bezpečný experiment | safe experiment | sicheres Experiment | “Experiment” is practical and non-clinical, but can sound scientific. | Keep unless the brand prefers “small safe try” / “sicherer Versuch”. |
| návrat k sobě | return to yourself | zu dir selbst zurückkehren | This is a core metaphor and several natural translations are possible. | Keep the warm, direct second-person form consistently. |

No item above blocks use of EN or DE: each has a complete first-pass translation and is only flagged for editorial consistency.
